LockViet

Learn Vietnamese from your lock screen.

LockViet puts two cards on your iPhone lock screen: a Vietnamese word with its meaning, and real phrases that use it. Every time you glance at your phone you learn a little. In the app you check yourself by typing words with their accent marks, and follow a roadmap of levels and topics.

Contact

Questions, a card you think is wrong, or a problem with a purchase: email support@lockviet.app. We read every message.

Adding the widgets

  1. Lock your iPhone, then press and hold the lock screen.
  2. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area under the clock.
  4. Add LockViet · Word, then LockViet · Examples beside it.

Tap either card to see the next word.

Subscriptions

The widgets and the first levels are free. LockViet Pro opens every level and topic. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open LockViet → Me → Restore purchases.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your learning progress stays on your iPhone. There's no account. See the Privacy Policy.
