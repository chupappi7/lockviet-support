Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

LockViet ("we", "us") is an iOS app made by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app uses and why.

No account

LockViet has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Your settings (dialect, track, pace), the words you have seen, your answers, your progress and your streak are stored only on your device, shared between the app and its widgets. We can't see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Pronunciation

"Hear it" uses the speech voices built into iOS on your device. No audio is recorded or uploaded.

Anonymous usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened, when a check is finished, and app version and device model. Events are tied to a random identifier created by the app, not to your identity, and are used only to improve LockViet. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription and Superwall to show the upgrade screen. They receive a random app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information, so Pro can be unlocked and restored. We never receive your payment details.

No tracking, no ads

LockViet does not show ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

LockViet is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete all local data by deleting the app. To ask about analytics or purchase data linked to your random ID, email support@lockviet.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
